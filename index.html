Hi! Lovely guys ~ Nice to meet you. I am introverted, but I also enjoy good friends. I'm Nash Aiomos, a software engineer and writer interested in Web3, blockchain, the metaverse, and AI.

I enjoy programming, reading, hiking, running, climbing, and playing video games. My reading interests range from science fiction and mystery novels to economics, history, business, psychology, and philosophy. When I’ve spent too much time indoors, I like to go for a walk or plan a short trip. Some of my favorite games are Grand Theft Auto V, Don’t Starve, and Sid Meier’s Civilization VI.

I’ve been exploring cryptocurrency and Web3 since 2020. I wrote about how I got started in my blog. I enjoy building web applications and working with blockchain technology, mainly using Rust, TypeScript, and JavaScript. I also write about technology and share what I learn through articles and open-source projects.

I started using AI tools in November 2022, and Claude and ChatGPT are now part of my everyday toolkit. I’m interested in AI agents, product experiences, and finding new ways to turn ideas into useful products.

I’m always learning and exploring how Web3, blockchain, the metaverse, and AI can shape useful products and new digital experiences.
